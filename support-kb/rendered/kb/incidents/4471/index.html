Separate problems

parser — the shared parser flips day-first dates

The shared parser reads month first. Tenants with day-first locales receive dates with the month and day reversed. This cause includes app, email and booking-v2 form reports.

Desk outcome: Fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

hint — booking-v3's date hint conflicts with day-first tenants

The booking-v3 form shows MM/DD/YYYY. People follow that hint, while the form reads the value using the tenant's day-first order. These form reports are a separate cause from the shared parser.

Desk outcome: Won't fix; the booking-v3 form was retired.

Tickets

  • parser (379 reports): Shared parser reads day-first dates as month-first.
  • hint (33 reports): Booking-v3 date hint conflicts with day-first tenants.

Lessons

  • One big ticket can hide multiple causes. Use the tenant locale and booking form to separate them.
  • The export overlaps. Count each report ID once, and do not use the intake tag as ground truth.
